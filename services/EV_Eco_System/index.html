About EV Charging

High-performance DC fast charging and turnkey programs built to scale with you.

Ampleton® now offers high-performance DC fast charging infrastructure for businesses, fleets and public networks across India. Whether you're a Charge Point Operator, fleet operator, or a business looking to integrate EV charging, we deliver complete turnkey solutions built to scale and grow with you.

Every Ampleton EV charging solution is carefully planned based on site conditions, power availability, and future scalability ensuring consistent charging speeds, grid stability and intelligent load management. From site assessment and installation to commissioning and AMC support, we handle everything end-to-end.

Ampleton DC Fast Charger Lineup

Ampleton 60

Output: 60 kW DC. Best for: High-traffic fleet & public charging environments. A smart 60kW DC fast charger with optional 22kW AC output combining flexibility, simultaneous multi-connector support, and reliable performance.

Ampleton 120

Output: 120 kW DC. Best for: Commercial fleets, public charging & high-utilization environments. Mid-power DC charger built for performance, compatibility, and smart load management.

Ampleton 180

Output: 180 kW DC. Best for: Semi-urban charging hubs, fleet depots & high-turnover locations. High-performance DC fast charger engineered for scalability and smart energy distribution balancing power and efficiency.

Ampleton 240

Output: 240 kW DC. Best for: Busy outdoor charging environments. Ultra-fast DC charger with dual connectors for simultaneous charging built for outdoor durability and high-capacity reliability.

Ampleton 320

Output: 320 kW DC. Best for: Ultra-fast multi-vehicle commercial charging at scale. High-power 320kW DC charger engineered for performance, modularity and user-focused design for demanding commercial applications.
