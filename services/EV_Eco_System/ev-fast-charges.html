EV Fast Chargers

Built for Speed. Engineered for Reliability.

Overview

Fast, reliable charging is the backbone of electric mobility, and Ampleton's EV fast charging solutions are engineered to deliver performance without compromise. From single-unit installations to multi-bay charging hubs, we design and deploy infrastructure that is purpose-built for long-term performance across a wide range of environments and use cases.

Every charging deployment begins with a deep site assessment covering power availability, usage patterns, load behaviour, and future scalability requirements. This ensures you receive a system that works effectively today and can be expanded as your needs grow, whether you operate a gated residential complex, a corporate campus, a commercial mall, a highway corridor, a fleet depot, or a public charging location.

What Makes Ampleton's EV Charging Different

Ampleton brings intelligent load management to every installation, preventing grid overload during peak demand periods while maintaining consistent charging performance. Each system is designed specifically for its site, accounting for sanctioned power load, peak usage windows, and the mix of vehicle types expected. Our chargers support all major charging standards including CCS2, CHAdeMO, and Type 2 AC, ensuring compatibility with the full range of EVs on Indian roads today.

Safety is built into every layer of the installation. Over-current, over-voltage, and thermal protection systems are standard across all deployments. Remote monitoring and real-time diagnostics through our smart management platform allow our team to identify and resolve issues proactively, minimizing downtime and maximising uptime for users. The systems are built for minimal on-site maintenance, keeping operational costs low over the full lifecycle of the infrastructure.

Our Deployment Process

A successful EV charging installation is not just about placing hardware; it requires disciplined engineering and project management from start to finish. Ampleton's deployment process begins with a thorough site survey and load assessment, followed by system design and equipment selection tailored to the site's unique requirements. Our team handles all civil and electrical infrastructure preparation, including cabling, earthing, and board upgrades where necessary. Charger installation and commissioning are carried out by certified engineers, followed by comprehensive testing, user training, and formal handover. Post-installation, our team remains engaged through preventive maintenance contracts and remote support so your charging infrastructure continues to perform at its best throughout its operational life.
