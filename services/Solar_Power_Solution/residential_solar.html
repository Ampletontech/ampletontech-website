Residential Solar

Power Your Home. Own Your Energy.

Overview

Ampleton's residential solar systems give homeowners the power to generate their own clean energy, reduce dependency on the grid and lock in long-term savings that compound year after year. Whether it is a standalone house, a villa or an apartment with a dedicated terrace, we design systems that fit your home precisely, not a generic template applied to every roof.

Every residential project begins with a detailed energy audit and roof suitability assessment, ensuring your system is sized correctly from the start. We do not oversize systems to inflate project value, nor do we undersize to reduce upfront cost. Our goal is to maximise your return on investment while delivering reliable performance across the full 25-year life of the panels.

What's Included

Ampleton's residential solar service covers the entire journey from initial assessment to post-installation support. We begin with a free home energy audit and roof assessment, followed by customised system design using Waaree panels optimised for your specific consumption profile. Our installation team handles everything, including mounting structures, wiring, inverter installation and metering, to deliver a clean, safe, and efficient system. We manage the net metering application and all DISCOM liaison work on your behalf, so you are not left navigating utility paperwork alone. Every system comes with a 25-year panel performance warranty, and our team continues to support you through annual maintenance reviews and performance monitoring to ensure your system continues to deliver as designed.

System Options

Depending on your requirements and location, Ampleton offers on-grid systems that allow you to sell surplus energy back to the grid, off-grid systems for full energy independence, and hybrid systems that combine grid connectivity with battery backup for uninterrupted power. All net-metered systems are configured to reduce your electricity bill to near zero, turning your roof into a long-term financial asset.

Why Homeowners Choose Ampleton

We are a full EPC partner meaning we own the entire process from design to commissioning and beyond. Our certified installation teams ensure every system is safe, compliant with DISCOM requirements, and built to last the full warranty period. Post-installation, our service team remains accountable through AMC agreements and performance monitoring so you always have a single point of contact for anything related to your solar system.
