WHO WE ARE

At Ampleton®, we are a service-based engineering & energy company driven by a team of passionate and experienced professionals. Since May 2018, we have specialized in comprehensive energy and infrastructure services, acting as consultants, designers, and execution partners across commercial, industrial and institutional sectors.

We focus on delivering efficient, reliable, and cost-effective solutions that empower businesses to optimize performance and achieve long-term sustainability.

OUR DEVELOPMENT COURSE

Our journey began with a simple belief that businesses can grow without harming the planet.

2018

The Beginning

A small team of engineers founded Ampleton with the vision to make quality Solar and Electrical solutions accessible to households and businesses across South India.

2019

Building Trust

Strengthened our technical capabilities and expanded support services, offering professional Electrical Contracting and Consultancy across Andhra Pradesh.

2020

Expanding Services

Introduced structured Solar EPC models, improved installation efficiency, and extended services to larger residential and commercial clients.

2021

Entered Commercial & Industrial Projects

Delivered major rooftop solar projects for commercial and industrial sectors, bringing scalable and reliable solutions to growing businesses.

2022

30kW & 50kW Commercial Projects

Successfully executed 30kW and 50kW commercial solar projects, marking our entry into medium-scale solar deployments and strengthening our commercial project portfolio.

2023

Authorized Partner with Waaree

Became an authorized partner with Waaree, one of India's leading solar manufacturers, ensuring premium quality panels, stronger supply chain, and enhanced product reliability for our clients.

2024

Expanding to Telangana & Offering Franchises

Expanded our business operations to Telangana and offered franchise opportunities, enabling partners to grow with Ampleton and extend our trusted energy solutions to more customers across the region.

2025

Milestone Achieved

Surpassed 1500+ successful projects, including a recently completed 1MW solar installation, earning a reputation for quality, precision, and customer-focused execution. Extended our branches across Karnataka, strengthening our presence across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana & Karnataka.

2026

The Road Ahead

Launched EV Charging Systems and continuing to innovate with advanced engineering solutions, stronger service networks, and future-ready energy systems that support India's evolving energy needs.

About Our Founder & CEO, Er. Mahaboob Subhan

Er. Mahaboob Subhan is the Founder and CEO of Ampleton Power Solutions Pvt. Ltd. A licensed electrical engineer with over 10 years of hands-on experience in the energy and infrastructure sector. His career has always been rooted in execution, building a deep technical foundation before stepping into business leadership. In 2018, he channelled that expertise into founding Ampleton Power Solutions with a clear mission: to help businesses and communities thrive through reliable and innovative energy solutions. Since then, Ampleton has grown into a Pan-India company operating across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Karnataka, backed by a strong team of skilled professionals delivering smart, scalable, and sustainable power solutions. From electrical advisory and strategic project planning to team management and complete end-to-end execution, Er. Mahaboob leads every dimension of the business with the precision of an engineer and the vision of an entrepreneur. As India moves toward a cleaner and more connected energy future, he continues to drive Ampleton forward expanding into renewable energy, EV charging infrastructure, and next-generation power systems that are built to last and designed to perform.
